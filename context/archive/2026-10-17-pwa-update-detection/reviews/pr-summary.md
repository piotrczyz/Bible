**What this delivers:** The installed app (added to your home screen) will start picking up new versions on its own — no more removing and re-adding it to get updates. Fully closing and reopening the app loads the newest version right away, with nothing to do. If a new version is published while you're already using the app, a small "new version available" message appears that you can tap whenever you're ready; it won't interrupt what you're reading.

**Please check on the preview:**
- Fully close the app from your home screen and reopen it after a new version has been published — it should just load the latest version with no message.
- With the app open, switch away to another app and back after a new version has been published — you should see the "new version available" message without the screen reloading on its own, and tapping it should load the new version.
- After both of the above, confirm your reading history, settings, last reading position and any downloaded offline Bible content are unchanged, and that the app still works with no internet connection.

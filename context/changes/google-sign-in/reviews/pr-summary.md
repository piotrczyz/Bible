**What this delivers:** Reading history now follows a signed-in user's
Google account instead of staying on one device: readings made on one phone
or browser show up on another once the user signs in there, and readings
made while offline are saved automatically once the connection comes back.
Signing out clears the reading history from that device's screen (it stays
safe on the account).

**Please check on the preview:**
- Read a few verses while signed in, then open the app signed in with the
  same Google account on another device or browser and confirm the same
  history shows up there
- Turn off your connection, read a few verses, turn the connection back on,
  and confirm those readings appear in your history without doing anything
  else
- Sign out and confirm your reading history disappears from that device,
  then sign back in and confirm it is still there
- Tap the reading-history icon while signed out and confirm you see the
  sign-in screen instead of your history, and that signing in takes you
  straight into the timeline

**Status:** Not ready yet: before this ships, we want to close a gap where,
on a shared device, if one person was signed in and went offline, then
signed out before reconnecting, and a second person then signs in with
their own Google account on that same device, the first person's
not-yet-saved readings could end up recorded under the second person's
account instead of being discarded. We're fixing that before this goes
further.
